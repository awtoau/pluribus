Bitmap legend and options
Verified = LUT INIT bits (compared with vendor netlists, 100 % over 301 builds). Evidence = a mapping exists without a per-bit oracle (LE-used bit, clock polarity, routing tables, translated routing specimens). Unknown = no meaning attributed. Evidence also covers table-only routing cells: known here means "has a proposed meaning", and is weaker than verified.
Die position
Notes

Tier: verified = LUT INIT bits checked against a vendor netlist (256 of a LAB tile's cells). Evidence = a LAB with measured routing specimens. Unknown = no bit attributed. Density = set bits of one example design binned to tiles; the row binning is approximate.

Row binning is by band [VY-68, VY+1] (70 v). Cells of classes 1, 2 and 3 are owned by the tile that generated them; the rest are binned by frame band and v row. Frames FX+16..FX+17 of a LAB column are shared with the next column band.

Chip overlay: ball matrix follows View (Bottom / Top); die orientation follows the Die position switch (top surface or flip-chip mirrored). Die size is schematic. Faint lines = every ball with a die site; strong = hovered, selected, searched, or same bank as the hovered ball. No lines for power / ground / NC (no site expected).
