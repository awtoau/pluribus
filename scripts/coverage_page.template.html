Reading these numbers

Verified = compared against a vendor oracle (LUT INIT vs the Quartus post-fit netlist; frame CRC-16 recomputed; header sync/CRC). Evidence = a mapping exists but no per-bit oracle compare: the classes marked decoded, not oracle-verified, plus constants. Unknown = nothing attributes a meaning. The header frame is split by its own counts (24 verified, 6,185 known or constant, 159 unknown); in chart 4 its set bits cannot be split and are shown as a separate mixed segment.

Geometry-only regions (M9K, multiplier, IO, periphery) are located by column and row position and are counted as unknown. The ledger is append-only: docs/coverage-history.jsonl; refresh with cycloneiv_bit_inventory.py --record.
