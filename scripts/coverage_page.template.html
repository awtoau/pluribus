Reading these numbers

Tier names are the claim tiers and statuses of docs/provenance-and-feedback.md; bits are grouped by them, never by one "evidence" bucket. Patterns are revocable: a missed corner case moves a class to suspect or refuted and its bits out of the verified tiers; a real lift that fails does the same to every pattern in its path. Not every cell is tested: a rule that passed the sampling standard stands, with its sample n and misses on the class row in section 5. The header frame is split by its own counts (24 verified, 6,185 measured or constant, 159 unknown); in chart 4 its set bits are shown as one mixed segment.

Geometry-only regions (M9K, multiplier, IO, periphery) are located by column and row position and are counted as unknown. The ledger is append-only: docs/coverage-history.jsonl; refresh with cycloneiv_bit_inventory.py --record.
